Privacy Policy for Bindings

Effective date: 28 October 2026

Bindings is a personal reading tracker. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it.

What Bindings stores

Your library — book titles, authors, genres, ratings, notes, quotes, reading dates, and cover images — is stored:

  • Locally on your device, and
  • In your own private iCloud account, if you're signed in, so your library can sync across your Mac, iPhone, and iPad.

This data is never sent to us, stored on our servers, or shared with any third party. We don't operate a server for Bindings at all — your library lives in Apple's iCloud, under your Apple ID, governed by Apple's own privacy practices (see Apple's Privacy Policy at apple.com/legal/privacy).

Looking up book details

When you look up a book by ISBN or scan its barcode, Bindings sends the ISBN number to Open Library and/or Google Books to retrieve the title, author, page count, and cover image. No other information about you or your library is sent with that request. If you've added your own Google Books API key in Settings, that key is stored only on your device — we never see it.

Barcode scanning

Barcode scanning uses your device's camera to read the barcode directly on-device. No photo or video is captured, stored, or sent anywhere — the camera feed is processed locally by iOS and discarded immediately after the barcode is recognised.

Importing and exporting your library

Bindings lets you export your library to a file (for backup) and import it back, or import from a Goodreads export file. These files are handled entirely on your device — you choose where they're saved, and we never see their contents.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect analytics or usage data.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising trackers.
  • We don't require you to create an account with us.
  • We don't sell or share your data with anyone, because we never have access to it in the first place.

Children's privacy

Bindings is not directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
