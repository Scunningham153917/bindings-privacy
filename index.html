Bindings Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Bindings is a reading tracker for iPhone, iPad and Mac. It has no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking. I (the developer) never receive any of your data.

Where your library is stored

Your books, shelves, notes and reading progress are stored on your device. If you're signed in to iCloud, they also sync through your own private iCloud storage, using Apple's CloudKit. Apple handles that storage under the Apple Privacy Policy, and I can't see it.

Book lookups (Google Books and Open Library)

Bindings only goes online when you ask it to look up a book. That happens when you:

  • tap Look Up in Add Book, or Find by ISBN in Edit Book
  • scan a barcode
  • tap Find Covers and Details in Settings

Each time, the app sends one of two things to Google Books, and to Open Library if Google has no result:

  • the book's ISBN, or
  • the book's title and author.

Nothing else from your library is sent. The information that comes back (cover, page count, author) is saved into your library only if those fields are blank.

Google and Open Library get these requests the same way any website does when you visit it. Their own privacy policies apply: Google Privacy Policy and Internet Archive (Open Library) terms.

Optional Google Books API key

If you add your own Google Books API key under Settings > Your Library > Advanced, it's stored only on that device. It's sent only to Google, as part of book lookups. You don't need a key to use Bindings.

Camera, photos and files

  • Barcode scanning uses the camera to read the barcode on your device. No images are saved or sent anywhere.
  • Cover pictures you choose with Photos or File stay in your library.
  • Goodreads, Kindle and StoryGraph import files are read on your device and are never uploaded.

Deleting your data

You can delete any book in the app. To remove everything, delete the app. To remove the iCloud copy too, go to Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Storage.

Children

Bindings doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.
